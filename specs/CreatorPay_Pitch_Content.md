# CreatorPay — Pitch Deck Content

**Purpose:** Concise content and visual direction for a 12-slide investor/judging deck. This is not the PowerPoint file. Keep the slides visually clean; use the accompanying `CreatorPay_Product.md` for context, assumptions, and fuller explanations.

## Presentation design system

- **Format:** 16:9 widescreen.
- **Style:** Editorial, sharp, confident, product-led. Large typography, short copy, substantial negative space, consistent alignment.
- **Palette (maximum three colours):** Deep ink/navy `#101828`, electric blue `#4263EB`, warm off-white `#F8FAFC`. Use colour to clarify structure, not as decoration.
- **Typography:** One strong sans-serif family with clear weight contrast. Large slide titles; body copy should remain comfortably readable from a distance.
- **Graphics:** Use simple vector shapes, cards, arrows, and product-interface mockups. Avoid stock photos, excessive gradients, emoji, and ornamental illustrations.
- **Rule:** The copy under **On-slide content** is the copy intended to appear on the slide. The **Visual direction** is an implementation note for the designer and should not be added as slide text unless explicitly stated.
- **Research discipline:** All market sizing below is a provisional model. Do not represent assumptions as verified facts. No customer, revenue, pilot, or partnership traction is claimed.

# Slide 2 — The Problem

### On-slide content

**Creator marketing is hard to account for.**

**01 — Results are unclear**  
Businesses struggle to connect creator activity to registrations, leads, and sales.

**02 — Campaign work is fragmented**  
Briefs, content, reporting, and payment records live across disconnected tools.

**03 — Compensation is difficult to verify**  
Businesses and creators lack one shared record of agreed work, attributable results, and rewards.

**Bottom line:** Businesses struggle to justify spend. Creators struggle to prove value.

### Visual direction

Three equal cards, each with a short headline and one sentence. Make the headline legible first. Do not put a story or supporting paragraph on the slide.

# Slide 3 — The Solution

### On-slide content

**One connected workflow for creator campaigns.**

Circular process:

1. **Define** — Set objectives, deliverables, attribution, and reward rules.
2. **Manage** — Invite creators, share briefs, collect submissions.
3. **Track** — Record clicks and supported conversion events.
4. **Verify** — Check evidence against campaign rules.
5. **Reward** — Calculate fees and performance rewards transparently.
6. **Settle** — Record approvals and payment status.
7. **Learn** — Compare outcomes and improve the next campaign.

### Visual direction

Use a circular or looped process graphic with directional arrows. Keep each step to a short label and one short description. Do not create seven separate paragraphs. The design should show that each stage feeds the next.

Use four market cards:

| Card | Headline | Supporting label |
|---|---:|---|
| **Global TAM** | **$180M** | Modeled annual software-revenue opportunity |
| **Global SAM** | **$18M** | Modeled reachable segment |
| **Global SOM** | **$360K–$540K ARR** | Three-year modeled capture: 200–300 paying accounts |
| **Nigeria SOM** | **$30K–$60K ARR** | Initial beachhead scenario: 25–50 paying accounts |

Small source/method note: **TAM/SAM/SOM are bottom-up planning scenarios, not published market totals. Account counts and pricing require validation. Global influencer-marketing spend is market context, not CreatorPay revenue.**

### How the model is calculated (not all of this belongs on the slide)

- **Global TAM:** 100,000 potential business/agency accounts × $1,800 assumed annual revenue per account = $180M.
- **Global SAM:** 10,000 high-fit accounts in initially serviceable markets × $1,800 assumed annual revenue per account = $18M.
- **Global SOM:** 200–300 paying accounts × $1,800 assumed annual revenue per account = $360,000–$540,000 ARR.
- **Nigeria SOM:** 25–50 paying accounts × $1,200 assumed annual revenue per account = $30,000–$60,000 ARR. Nigerian pricing may be lower than international pricing.

### Source

Statista's worldwide influencer-marketing market series estimated the 2025 market at $32.55B: [Statista — Influencer marketing market size worldwide](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1092819/global-influencer-market-size/).

### Visual direction

Use four cards in a consistent grid. Keep the numbers dominant and labels secondary. Include one short methodology note at the bottom in a readable size. Do not visually imply that the $32.55B industry-spend number is software revenue available to CreatorPay.

# Slide 6 — Business Model

### On-slide content

**Two revenue streams. One business customer focus.**

**01 — Business subscriptions**  
Recurring monthly or annual plans for businesses managing creator campaigns, tracking results, and handling reward workflows.

**02 — Agency plans**  
Paid workspaces for agencies managing campaigns and reporting across multiple client businesses.

**Core principle:** Creators can participate in campaigns and view their own results and reward status without buying a subscription.

### Visual direction

Two large cards only. Do not add API fees, campaign usage fees, transaction commissions, or unvalidated price tiers as separate revenue streams. API access is a product capability within the appropriate plan, not a third revenue stream in this strategy.

# Slide 8 — Competitive Landscape

### On-slide content

**The category exists. Our wedge is the campaign accountability record.**

| Competitor | Publicly stated strength | CreatorPay's intended distinction |
|---|---|---|
| **AdMinting** | Performance-led influencer campaigns, click/lead/conversion tracking, creator scoring | Make agreed terms, evidence, reward calculations, and settlement history one explicit record |
| **Posse** | African-market creator discovery, campaign workflows, platform data, analytics, AI insights | Serve brands that bring their existing creators and need transparent reward/accountability workflows |
| **Beza** | Creator discovery, campaign management, performance tracking, payments/escrow positioning | Keep fixed deliverable compensation and performance rewards distinct, rules-based, and auditable |
| **Modash** | Creator discovery, content tracking, analytics, Shopify sales attribution, creator payment tools | Focus the product on the shared commercial record and flexible evidence methods, including API-led event reporting |
| **Spreadsheets / agencies** | Familiar tools and managed campaign execution | Reduce repeated reconciliation by connecting terms, work, evidence, and reward status |

**Our intended advantage:** An auditable chain from campaign terms → delivered work → attributable evidence → reward calculation → settlement record.

### Visual direction

Keep competitors in a compact table or four comparison cards. Acknowledge overlap honestly. Do not claim that competitors lack features unless verified through product testing. CreatorPay's position is a strategic hypothesis to validate, not a proven moat.
